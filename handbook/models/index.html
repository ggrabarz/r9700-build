    Models¶

    The model decision is a residency decision. Decode is DRAM-bound below M ≈ 300 rows/GEMM, so the only question that matters is how much of the model lives in VRAM. Everything else — engine, quant, topology — follows from that.

    2. Qwen3.8-27B — the clean win¶

    Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration · H=5120 · 64 layers · 24 Q / 4 KV heads · head_dim 256 · intermediate 17408 · vocab 248,320 · hybrid linear_attention/full_attention · MTP 1 layer.

    Quantity Value
    BF16 checkpoint 55.56 GB
    FP8 active weights 27.8 GB
    KV 64 KiB/token
    At 262,144 tokens, TP=2, FP8 KV 4.0 GiB per card
    BF16 weights + BF16 KV at the same profile ~35.8 GB/card — does not fit

    Why it is the default: it is the only model with published, independently measured, end-to-end numbers on this exact silicon, and it is the only one that yields two engines per four-card box. FP8 is load-bearing: the FP8 checkpoint and FP8 KV are what make 262 K context fit.

    Configuration: Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8, vLLM-Radiance, TP=2 per engine × 2 engines, FP8 KV, MTP on, ≤8 concurrent per engine.

    The measured anchor: 493 written tok/s across 12 cards / 3 nodes on real jobs (41.1 tok/s/GPU); one quad at 623 tok/s and 837 W for 16 users; $18.15/Mtok on real jobs.

    The catch: 4 KV heads mean TP > 4 replicates the KV cache, and vLLM refuses Qwen3.8-27B at TP=8 by name — there is no gqa3 in the attention lattice {1,2,4,6,8,12}. The octo cannot use its headline feature on the proven model.

    3. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next — the flagship MoE¶

    Qwen4ExpForConditionalGeneration · H=2560 · 48 layers = 12 × (3 × Gated DeltaNet → MoE, 1 × Qwen Sparse Attention → MoE) · GDN 48 V / 16 QK heads · QSA 24 Q / 2 KV heads, head_dim 256, RoPE 64 · QSA indexer MQA, budget 512 blocks / 2,048 tokens · 512 experts, 10 routed + 1 shared · n-gram embedding 20,000,000 bigrams/trigrams at layer 2 · Gated Residual 4 branches · vocab 248,320 · context 262,144 native → 1,000,000 · MTP 1 layer · FP8 block [128,128].

    The n-gram table is the whole problem. 51 B params is 102 GB at fp16, ~25 GB at 3-bit. Upstream vLLM's n-gram offload is NVIDIA-only. The table must live in VRAM, in host RAM, or on SSD behind an engine that supports it.

    That is what the PLE table is for. Strata's prompt-lookup-expert table on SSD is the mechanism built for exactly this, and it is measured working on the R9700 (982 tok/s prompt, Coder IQ1_M). The placement matters more than the medium:

    PLE placement Effect
    On NVMe with an 8 GB RAM cache (B45) 5.2–6.3 k prefill
    In host RAM (3-card box, 256 K fp8 context) 5–7 k → 24 k prefill, 150–220 tok/s decode
    ~51 GB host RAM reserved (B26) 102.2 → 437.8 tok/s c1→c8
    47.7 GiB pinned (B14) the 236 → 1,192 tok/s monolith

    Fits: on four cards only at low bit-width. At the octo comparison point it has 96.4 GB of headroom on 8 cards against a 31.62 GB deficit on 4. This is the one genuinely octo-shaped workload — but it needs a measured TP=8 path that does not exist yet.

    Engine: standalone Radiance with MXFP4/Quark containers (measured ~75 tok/s on 2 × R9700), or Strata when streaming.

    4. GLM-5.3-Flash — the best perf-per-dollar MoE¶

    Glm5NextForConditionalGeneration (glm5_next): 320 B total / 18 B active, 45 layers, 288 routed + 1 shared, top-8, sigmoid router with noaux_tc, routed_scaling_factor 2.5, hidden 4096, vocab 154,880, context 1,048,576.

    It is a hybrid: 3 dense-MLP lead layers + 34 KDA (gated delta-net) linear-attention layers + 11 deepseek_sparse_attention (MLA + DSA) layers. The MLA has qk_rope_head_dim = 0 — there is no rope_theta in the Flash config at all. Positional information reaches the model only through the KDA layers.

    Quantity Value
    Cache per token per layer 768 elements
    At 256 K, Q8_0 1.18 GiB per card (vs 4.43 GiB f32 on two cards)
    KDA state 142 MiB per sequence, constant
    3-bit GSQ-RCO pack 117.5 GB — fits 4 × 32 GB entirely
    8-card TP=8 KV need at 8 × 262,144 33.0 GiB/rank vs a 9.1 GiB budget

    Why it is the best tokens-per-dollar configuration: Strata-GLM-AMD measured 30–39 tok/s decode on 2 × W7900 (96 GB) and found it bound by host RAM bandwidth (~42 GB/s dual-channel DDR4). On a 4 × 32 GB box the 117.5 GB pack fits entirely in VRAM, so no expert ever touches the host RAM bus. The binding constraint is removed, and the cache is cheap.

    But the octo verdict still says no: GLM-5.3-Flash at --tp 8 is 53.25 tok/s/GPU, +29.6 % over the quad's 41.08 — and the break-even BOM for that gain is $27,807, above the cheapest orderable octo. TP=4 is impossible because the MLA latent is replicated. On 8 cards the realistic profile is 2 × 256 K or 8 × 128 K, not 8 × 262 K.

    5. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash — the MoE with the best AMD story, and the worst fit¶

    DeepseekV41ForCausalLM · Causal Encoder–Decoder: 40 layers = 20-layer causal encoder + 20-layer decoder · CSA2 in every layer except the first two encoder layers (SWA) · Hierarchical Sparse Indexer (32 heads, head_dim 128, top-k 512) · Single-Pass mHC, four residual streams · Engram conditional memory: two tables of 384 M embeddings at layers 1 and 14 → 196 B params · DSpark speculative decoding (3 nextn layers) · H=5120 · 384 routed experts, 6 active + 1 shared · FP8 dense (block 32×32, ue8m0) with expert_dtype: fp4 · context 1,048,576 with YaRN · checkpoint 510.31 GB in 48 shards.

    The KV is tiny: CSA2's cross-layer reuse + FP4 KV + SWA bring the global KV to 890 bytes/token. At 1 M tokens that is 890 MB, not tens of gigabytes. The pressure is entirely in the weights.

    The viable route is Strata, not vLLM. The 510 GB FP8 checkpoint through vLLM cpu_offload is not viable; Engram alone is 183 GiB of FP8, larger than total host RAM on the proven box. The Strata ISTA packs (239–274 GB, 46–64 GB RAM) are the route actually tested on four R9700s.

    Datapoint Result
    B7, 4 × R9700 with affinity, 2.875 bpw 79 tok/s, all 11,008 experts resident
    B43, 4 × R9700 + 251 GB RAM 6,197 experts in VRAM / 9,163 on CPU; Coder 59 tok/s (26 on one card)
    B49, Gen3 x4 no-P2P host 20.15 tok/s
    B6, IQ2_XS prefill-bound; Strata 140–160 vs Radiance 235 on Flash-Next Q4

    The lesson: the "all experts resident" configuration is the one that works. Residency break-even is ≥34.2 % of routable active-expert bytes for 20 tok/s; on the proven single-drive tier it needs ≥91.8 % — unreachable. That is why the reference box serves Qwen and not DeepSeek.

    6. Kimi K3 — a capacity product, not a serving product¶

    KimiK3ForConditionalGeneration (MoonViT-V2 vision tower) wrapping KimiLinearForCausalLM: 93 layers = 69 × KimiDeltaAttention + 24 × KimiMLAAttention; H=7168, 96 heads; 896 routed + 2 shared experts, top-16; ~2.78 T total / ~104 B active; LatentMoE with routed_expert_hidden_size 3584 (each expert 3 × 3584 × 3072).

    The official release is MXFP4: 96 shards, 1,560,936,091,448 bytes, experts only in MXFP4 (group 32), everything else BF16/F32. RDNA4 has no MXFP4 tensor op, so every expert matmul dequantises to FP16/int8 first — the same wall as DeepSeek's FP4 experts.

    Quantity on the proven quad Value
    Max residency of the 330 GB GGUF ~35–39 % (≈0 % at the production profile)
    Decode ceiling, NVMe-cold 0.2 tok/s
    Decode ceiling, theoretical expert-sparse 2.9 tok/s
    Shipped Qwen on the same box ~164 tok/s
    Gap 57× to 860× slower
    Native 1.56 TB checkpoint vs 931 GB formatted drive does not fit
    8 × 262,144 KV per TP rank 57.4 GiB — over a 32 GB card before weights

    Do not plan around it. If the requirement is Kimi, the answer is a different machine, not a different quantisation. The smallest GGUF (330 GB) is already larger than 256 GB of VRAM.

    7. Model-selection rules¶

    1. Fit first. Compute weights + KV × context × concurrency per card. If it exceeds ~29.7 GiB, lower the bit-width or the context — not the engine.
    2. Dense → TP=2 groups. MoE that fits → TP=4/8. MoE that does not → layer split.
    3. Keep the router in higher precision than the experts. Every low-bit MoE pack here does.
    4. Budget host RAM for the PLE / n-gram / Engram table before blaming the cards. It is worth 3–4× prefill.
    5. A bigger GPU does not lower the RAM a streamed model needs. RAM ≥ experts + ~10 GB.
    6. Skip the 2.4 T class entirely. 19× VRAM is not a quantisation problem.
