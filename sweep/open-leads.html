    How to attack the backlog cheaply¶

    1. posts_count first. Most L1T leads need a one-request check, not a re-read.
    2. Search by newest reply, not by known thread. search.json?q=R9700 order:latest found B46 after post-count re-reads had gone stale.
    3. Walk site indexes (/api/recent, board pages) rather than keyword search — that is how B45 and B49 were found on arca.live and lcz.me.
    4. Read author history. B44 came from author=Eroji, not from a title search.
    5. Retry 422s three times with ~3 s spacing before writing a venue off.
